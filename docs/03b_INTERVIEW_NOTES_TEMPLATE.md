# 03b. Standardized Interview Notes Template

> **Instructions for the Beginner PM:** Copy this template block for each of your 12–15 interviews. Keep raw notes factual, write direct quotes, and do not interpret or filter thoughts during the conversation.

---

```markdown
## Participant ID: P__ (e.g., P01)
- **Date / Time:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Target Role(s):** (e.g., Junior Software Engineer, Associate PM, Data Analyst)
- **Current Status:** (e.g., Final-year undergrad, graduated 6 months ago, career switcher)
- **Estimated Applications Submitted (Past 3 mos):** [  ] <10  [  ] 10–30  [  ] 30–75  [  ] 75+

---

### 1. Current System & Behavior
- **Primary tool used:** (e.g., Google Sheet, Notion, Apple Notes, Email search only, None)
- **How consistently updated:** (e.g., Real-time upon applying, batch on weekends, abandoned after 2 weeks)
- **Reason for friction / abandonment (if any):**
  > "[Direct quote from user regarding why they stopped or struggled]"

### 2. Must-Have vs. Ignored Fields
- **Fields they actually care about & record:** (e.g., Company, Role, Status, Date)
- **Fields they consider useless or rarely fill in:** (e.g., Job URL, contact person, salary range)

### 3. Critical Deadlines (Assessments / Interviews)
- **How they handle OA/interview deadlines:** (e.g., Google Calendar, sticky notes, mental math, starred emails)
- **Any missed or near-missed deadlines? Story:**
  > "[Direct quote or story describing the incident]"

### 4. Recruiter Outreach & Context Loss
- **Experience when contacted after weeks:** (e.g., Completely forgot applying, had to scramble to find job spec, seamless)

### 5. Follow-ups
- **Do they follow up?** [  ] Yes  [  ] No  [  ] Only after interview
- **Method / timing:** (e.g., "7 days after applying on LinkedIn", "Never, feel it annoys recruiters")

### 6. Experience with Existing Tools (Teal, Huntr, Simplify, Sheets, Notion)
- **Tools tried:**
- **Likes:**
- **Complaints / blockers:** (e.g., Paywall, extension felt creepy, too complicated, slow)

---

### Core Insight & PM Takeaway (1–2 sentences)
- *What is this participant's single biggest unresolved friction point?*
```
